## 🚀 Projects

### 🏦 1. Loan Data Analysis Dashboard

An interactive dashboard created to explore loan applications and
understand the factors associated with loan approval and rejection.

#### 🔍 What the dashboard analyzes

-   Total loan applicants
-   Approved and rejected applications
-   Loan approval ratio
-   Applicant gender
-   Education status
-   Credit history
-   Self-employment status
-   Property area
-   Number of dependents
-   Marital status
-   Applicant and co-applicant income
-   Loan amount
-   Loan amount by approval status

#### 📌 Key Dashboard Metrics

  Metric                      Value
  ------------------------ --------
  Total Applicants              614
  Total Approved                422
  Total Rejected                192
  Loan Approval Ratio        68.73%
  Total Amount Requested        87K

#### 💡 What I learned

This project helped me understand how different applicant
characteristics can be explored through data visualization. I also
practiced building KPIs, interactive charts, filters, and DAX-based
calculations to make the dashboard more useful for analysis.

📁 **Project folder:** [`Project-1`](./Project-1)

### 👗 2. Fashion Store Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard

An interactive retail dashboard created to understand fashion store
sales, customer purchasing patterns, product performance, ratings, and
payment methods.

#### 🔍 What the dashboard analyzes

-   Total customers
-   Items purchased
-   Total purchase amount
-   Average customer rating
-   Product-wise purchases
-   Monthly purchase trends
-   Product-wise review ratings
-   Purchase amount by payment method
-   Customer count by payment method

#### 📌 Key Dashboard Metrics

  Metric                        Value
  ----------------------- -----------
  Total Customers               3,400
  Total Items Purchased            50
  Total Purchase Amount     \$430,952
  Average Rating                 3.00

#### 💡 What I learned

This project helped me explore retail data from a customer and sales
perspective. I worked with different visualizations to identify
purchasing patterns, compare payment methods, understand product
performance, and present sales trends in a clear way.

📁 **Project folder:** [`Project-2`](./Project-2)
